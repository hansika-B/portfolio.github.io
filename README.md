# Hansika - Personal Engineering Portfolio (Espresso & Peony Edition)

A high-motion, dynamic personal portfolio website designed for **Hansika**, Computer Science Engineering Undergraduate (2024–2028) at **Vignana Bharathi Institute of Technology (VBIT)**, Hyderabad.

Inspired by the editorial luxury palette of **Espresso (`#3E2723`)** and **Peony Pink (`#F4C9D6`)**.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Aesthetic

- **Espresso** (`#3E2723`): Deep roasted cocoa velvet background in dark mode, and high-contrast typography in light mode.
- **Peony** (`#F4C9D6`): Luminous blush pink accents, glowing buttons, interactive particles, and badge borders.
- **Editorial Typography**: Elegant serif display headers (`Playfair Display`) paired with clean engineering typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans` & `JetBrains Mono`).

---

## ✨ Dynamic & Interactive Features

1. **Interactive Particle Canvas**: Floating Peony particles with gentle connecting constellation lines that react to cursor movement (repulsion physics).
2. **Dynamic 3D Tilt Cards**: Project cards tilt smoothly in 3D perspective based on mouse cursor position.
3. **Custom Glowing Cursor**: Sleek spring-interpolated trailing cursor dot and ring with hover expansions.
4. **Interactive Category Filter**: One-click filtering between *All Projects*, *Web Platforms*, *UI/UX & Cafe*, and *Live Deployed*.
5. **Dynamic Stats Banner**: Highlights graduation timeline (`2024–28`), core languages (`3+`), and live platforms (`3+`).
6. **Live Slot for Your Other Website**: Pre-configured 3rd project card ready with a "Live Website" direct link button and spec modal.
7. **Zero References**: References section completely removed in compliance with instructions.

---

1. **Smart Campus Query & Help-Desk System**:
